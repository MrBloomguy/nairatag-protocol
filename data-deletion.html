User Data Deletion Instructions

NairaTag Network is committed to user data autonomy and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

To request deletion of your phone number, WhatsApp link, Telegram chat association, or off-chain notifications:

  1. Send an email to privacy@nairatag.ng with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include your phone number or claimed ₦Tag handle for identity verification.
  3. Our team will process the purge of all off-chain records within 48 business hours and confirm via email.

Note: Onchain transactions previously broadcast to the decentralized Base blockchain cannot be modified or deleted due to the cryptographic nature of distributed ledgers.

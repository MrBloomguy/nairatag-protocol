Questions, answered

Start with the name.
Go wherever the protocol takes you.

A short guide to the relationship between TagProtocol, NairaTag, and the products around them.

What is TagProtocol?

TagProtocol is an open naming protocol for onchain identity. It provides the registry and resolution layer that lets names point to wallet addresses, payment endpoints, and useful application records.

What is NairaTag?

NairaTag is the first flagship product built on TagProtocol. It is the user-facing place to claim, manage, resolve, and use a human-readable onchain identity.

Can another app use TagProtocol names?

Yes. Wallets, contracts, payment products, and other applications can read the public registry and resolver records to support TagProtocol names in their own experiences.

What can I do with a NairaTag?

You can associate a name with your wallet, receive payments by name, publish records for applications, and use the identity across products that support TagProtocol.

Where can I learn how to integrate?

Use the developer section for the Chain 901 connection details, example resolver calls, deployed contract references, and the live query playground.

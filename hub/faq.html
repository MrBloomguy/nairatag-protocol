What is a NairaTag (₦Tag) and how does it work on Celo? Celo
▶

NairaTag is the sovereign naming protocol and autonomous AI agent network built natively on Celo Mainnet. It transforms long, cryptic 42-character hex addresses into intuitive handles like ₦SEGUN or ₦CHOWDECK.

When you register a ₦Tag, your identity is minted on-chain into the NairaTag Celo registry smart contract. It maps directly to your Celo wallet, allowing anyone to send you cNGN stablecoin payments without hexadecimal address friction.

Why is NairaTag built on Celo, and how do cNGN gas fees work? Celo
▶

Celo offers sub-second block finality, carbon-negative operations, and native support for multi-token gas fees via CIP-64. On Celo, transaction fees are paid directly in cNGN (the digital Naira stable token at address 0xe2702bd97ee33c88c8f6f92da3b733608aa76f71) or subsidized through gasless relays.

Transactions cost fractions of a cent (sub-0.01 cNGN), making micro-payments, everyday food orders, and high-frequency agent interactions economically practical.

How does NairaTag function as a MiniApp in Opera MiniPay & Telegram? MiniApp
▶

No separate app download is needed. NairaTag is designed as an Everywhere MiniApp:

• Opera MiniPay: Embedded directly as an HTML5 Web3 MiniApp for tens of millions of smartphone users across Nigeria and Africa with instant one-tap wallet access.
• Telegram Bot: Access your ₦Tag, query balances, and send funds directly via @NairaTagBot.
• WhatsApp Flow: Interact with your agent and resolve payment tags via WhatsApp messages.

Your single ₦Tag identity synchronizes effortlessly across all these channels.

What is a NairaTag Agent, and what can I do with my agent? AI Agents
▶

Every claimed ₦Tag comes paired with a personal, sovereign AI Agent powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash. Here is what your agent can do for you:

• Chat in Plain Language: Instruct your agent in plain English or Nigerian Pidgin (e.g., "Pay ₦CHOWDECK ₦4,500 for food" or "Show my recent settlements").

What is the Resolver Layer and how do handles translate to addresses? Resolver
▶

The Resolver Layer is the decentralized directory smart contract on Celo. When a user or app enters a tag like ₦SEGUN or a verified phone number, the resolver translates it into the recipient's Celo wallet address, payment endpoint, and escrow parameters with sub-second response times.

Any fintech, dApp, or merchant checkout can resolve ₦Tags with one line of code using our public SDK or Celo smart contract call resolveTag(string).

How do Send, Pay, and Smart Contract Escrow work? Payments
▶

Send & Pay allows direct, instant peer-to-peer transfers using human handles. Simply enter ₦TAG and the amount—no hexadecimal addresses or network gas configuration required.

Smart Contract Escrow protects commercial transactions and marketplace bounties. When you fund a task or purchase a service, funds are locked safely on Celo in a smart escrow contract. They are only released to the seller or solving agent after the deliverable is verified and approved by the creator.

Who controls my private keys and funds? Is NairaTag non-custodial? Security
▶

You do. NairaTag is 100% non-custodial. Neither our servers nor your AI agent ever have custody or access to your private keys or seed phrase.

When you ask your agent to register a tag, send payment, or lock escrow, the agent prepares unsigned transaction calldata. You review and sign all transactions directly in your connected wallet (Opera MiniPay, MetaMask, or social login wallet).

▶

₦Tags are unique on-chain identities issued on a first-come, first-served basis.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Effective Date: Immediate

1. Overview & Commitment

NairaTag Network ("NairaTag", "we", "us", or "our") operates a sovereign digital identity and payment routing protocol designed for Nigeria's onchain and digital economy. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy in full compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and international privacy standards.

Our Core Principle: NairaTag is built with self-custody and data minimization at its core. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not store private keys or sensitive passwords.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with NairaTag through our web application (nairatag.ng), WhatsApp Business Bot, Telegram Assistant, or smart contracts, we collect minimal necessary data to provide our identity routing services:

  • Handle Data: The human-readable handle you claim (e.g. ₦tag, ₦chioma).
  • Onchain Addresses: Your public wallet address (e.g. Celo Sepolia or Celo Mainnet) bound to your handle.
  • Messaging Identifiers: Your WhatsApp phone number or Telegram chat ID when you link your account for claims and notifications.
  • Virtual Account Metadata: Account number and bank name provisioned through our licensed banking partner (Flutterwave) for direct fiat deposits.

3. How We Use WhatsApp Data

When you interact with our official WhatsApp Business Assistant or WhatsApp Flows:

  • Your phone number is verified via WhatsApp's end-to-end encrypted session to bind your sovereign ₦Tag.
  • We process incoming commands (e.g., "Check name", "Claim", "Send payment") solely to execute the requested action.
  • We do not share your WhatsApp chat history, profile picture, or personal contacts with third parties.
  • All WhatsApp Flow interactions utilize RSA-OAEP and AES-128-GCM encrypted endpoints as mandated by Meta Cloud API security standards.

4. Blockchain & Public Ledger Records

Please note that by design, transactions submitted to the Base blockchain (such as handle minting or onchain transfers) are published to a public ledger. Public blockchain records are immutable, decentralized, and outside the direct control of any single entity.

5. Partner Integrations

We work with trusted, licensed financial and infrastructure providers:

  • Flutterwave: To issue Dedicated Virtual Accounts (NUBAN) and settle fiat payment requests in compliance with Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) regulations.
  • Meta Platforms Inc.: To deliver verified WhatsApp Business messaging and WhatsApp Flows.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS 1.3, AES-256) to safeguard data in transit and at rest. You have the right to request deletion of your messaging links or off-chain notifications at any time by contacting our data protection officer.
